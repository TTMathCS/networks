# How networks work

*A full course on computer networks, from what a network is to the research frontier, in pages of five to six minutes. It follows the path of the classic courses (Kurose and Ross's top-down approach, Tanenbaum's *Computer Networks*, Peterson and Davie's systems approach, Stanford's CS 144) but goes bottom-up, from signals to applications, so every layer rests on the one below.*

**You need:** the six [Start](../../start/index.md) lessons, or the same ideas from elsewhere. The math used is
explained where it appears; the [Math foundations](../math/index.md) book goes deeper. Each chapter has a matching
lab where you can see the idea on your own computer.
