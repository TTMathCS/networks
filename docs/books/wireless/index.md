# Wireless and mobile

*Radio links, from the physics of a wave to Wi-Fi 7, 5G and the Deep Space Network, in pages of five to six minutes. The structure follows the standard wireless courses (Goldsmith's *Wireless Communications*, Tse and Viswanath's *Fundamentals of Wireless Communication*, Rappaport's *Wireless Communications*) and the practical Wi-Fi curriculum used for wireless network certifications, but every claim is checked against the standards themselves.*

**You need:** the first chapters of [How networks work](../networks/index.md). The math (decibels, Fourier, constellations)
is in [Math foundations](../math/index.md), chapters 5–8.
